title: 'BayesSum: Bayesian Quadrature in Discrete Spaces'
abstract: This paper addresses the challenging computational problem of estimating
  intractable expectations over discrete domains.  Existing approaches, including
  Monte Carlo and Russian Roulette estimators, are consistent but often require a
  large number of samples to achieve accurate results. We propose a novel estimator,
  \emph{BayesSum}, which is an extension of Bayesian quadrature to discrete domains.
  It is more sample efficient than alternatives due to its ability to make use of
  prior information about the integrand through a Gaussian process. We show this through
  theory, deriving a convergence rate significantly faster than Monte Carlo in a broad
  range of settings. We also demonstrate empirically that our proposed method does
  indeed require fewer samples on several synthetic settings as well as for parameter
  estimation for Conway-Maxwell-Poisson and Potts models.
