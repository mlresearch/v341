title: Physics-informed machine learning for wind turbulence reconstruction with lidar
  under aircraft motion
abstract: We propose a physics-informed machine learning method for reconstructing
  3d turbulent wind from partial, noisy  and accumulated over time  observations obtained
  via lidar measurements. This method, which is optimal in terms of mean squared error,
  combines Gaussian process regression with a physics-informed kernel that leverages
  prior knowledge of the turbulence structure, as provided by the von Karman turbulence
  model.
