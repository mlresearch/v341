title: Scaling up Probabilistic PDE Simulators with Structured Volumetric Information
abstract: Modeling real-world problems with partial differential equations (PDEs)
  is a prominent topic in scientific machine learning. Classic solvers for this task
  continue to play a central role, e.g. to generate training data for deep learning
  analogues. Any such numerical solution is subject to multiple sources of uncertainty,
  both from limited computational resources and limited data (including unknown parameters).
  Gaussian process analogues to classic PDE simulation methods have recently emerged
  as a framework to construct fully probabilistic estimates of all these types of
  uncertainty. So far, much of this work focused on theoretical foundations, and as
  such is not particularly data efficient or scalable. Here we propose a framework
  combining a discretization scheme based on the well-known Finite Volume Method with
  complementary numerical linear algebra techniques. Practical experiments, including
  a spatiotemporal tsunami simulation, demonstrate substantially improved scaling
  behavior of this approach over previous collocation-based techniques.
